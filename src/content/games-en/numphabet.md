## Features

- A=1 … Z=26. Tap the number cards that become the word's letters, left to right
- 60-second Score Attack with a combo multiplier that climbs to ×2.00 at 20 in a row
- Three difficulties: Normal adds calculation cards (9+5) and reversed cards (1 becomes Z); Hard adds combined cards that calculate, then reverse
- Every board carries at least four decoys, most within one or two of a real answer
- Daily Challenge: five puzzles a day matched to the difficulties you have unlocked, with medals and a streak
- Friend battles in a private room, first to five rounds, with no stamina used
- Best scores and leaderboards per difficulty, detailed records, and a shareable result image

Play free with the Gumdrop theme, or pick up Prism Glass or Candy Sky, one-time purchases that each change the look, wordmark, music and sound. Free to download with in-app purchases and no subscriptions. For iPhone and iPad, in English and Japanese.
