## Features

- An endless magical block breaker where a curse wall steadily advances
- Collect mana from broken blocks and choose upgrades from three spell cards
- Five spells: Fire Burst, Chain Lightning, Ice Bind, Phantom Ball, and Arcane Beam
- Upgrade spells 12 times to split the normal ball into two
- Keep rallies and spell combinations going to chase the high-score ranking

## How to Play

- **Move**: Mouse movement / Left and right arrows / A and D
- **Start and launch**: Space / Enter / Left click
- **Spell cards**: Choose with the arrow keys or WASD, then press Enter. You can also click a card or use 1, 2, and 3
- **Pause and resume**: Esc / The pause button in the top-right corner
- **Retry**: R

Keep the magic ball in play, break through the ever-stronger curse wall, and aim for the highest score.
