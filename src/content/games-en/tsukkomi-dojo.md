## Features

- A manzai comedy game where you deliver the tsukkomi (the comeback) to your partner Masaru's gags with your own voice
- An AI judges how precise your comeback is; pinpoint the absurdity and add a clever twist for top marks
- Nail it and the hall bursts out laughing; miss and the room goes silent—sketch comedy on a traditional variety-hall stage
- After all seven gags, the judges give an overall rating. Aim for the title "Best Tsukkomi in the Land"

## How to Play

- **Play by voice**: After each gag, speak your comeback into the microphone. Open the game with "別タブで開く" (open in a new tab) on the unityroom page and allow microphone access
- **Play by text**: Type your comeback in the box that appears after each gag and press Enter. Timing is measured from when you start typing
- **Play again**: "もう一度" or "タイトルへ" on the results screen

About 3 minutes per run. If you can't speak aloud, you can type instead.

An AI is used to judge your comebacks. The text of your comeback (or the recognized text, when using voice) is sent to a judging server.

Recommended for Chrome on desktop. The game itself is in Japanese, and voice recognition is Japanese only.
