## Features

- A 60-second score attack with three cups pouring at the same time
- Judge coffee strength by color alone, without numbers or gauges
- Multitask across left, center, and right customer orders
- Online high-score ranking

## How to Play

- **Left lane**: Hold D to pour, then release to serve
- **Center lane**: Hold Space to pour, then release to serve
- **Right lane**: Hold K to pour, then release to serve

Match each requested shade and complete as many orders as possible in 60 seconds.
