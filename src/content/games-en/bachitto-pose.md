## Features

- Read the approaching silhouette and match it using one of four poses
- New pose choices with every wall; consecutive passes make the walls faster
- A clean pass earns a satisfying flash; a mistake sends you tumbling backward into the water
- Drum rolls and applause bring a TV game-show atmosphere to the stage
- Online leaderboard for consecutive walls cleared

## How to Play

- **Choose a pose**: ↑ / ← / ↓ / →, or click a pose card at the bottom
- **Start / Retry**: Space / Enter, or the on-screen button
- **Return to title**: Esc on the results screen
- **Toggle sound**: M or the sound button at the top right

Match the hole before the wall reaches you and try to beat your longest streak.

Recommended for desktop browsers. The game itself is in Japanese.
